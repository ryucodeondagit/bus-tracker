Method

  1. Collected King County Metro GTFS-realtime trip updates and vehicle positions every 30 seconds on a Google Cloud VM.
  2. Matched each departure to the GTFS schedule and measured the delay. 93% of departure times matched the bus GPS within 60 seconds.
  3. Calculated on-time rates and cluster bootstrap 95% confidence intervals in R.

Transit scheduling, geographic, and real-time data provided by permission of King County.
